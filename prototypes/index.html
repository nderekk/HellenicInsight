PoliticalBro front-page prototypes

Five takes on the same front page, all fed by the same synthetic news day (shared/data.js), so you can compare them like for like. Each one has a working Greek / English toggle.

  1. Election Night

    The news day as a results broadcast on a midnight-navy field: a results board for the lead event, outlet rows with swing bars, blindspots panel and a scrolling ticker. Built and reviewed.

    OpenStill

  2. Metro Line

    Athens Metro wayfinding: Greek over English sign panels, each event a departure with its own Left-to-Right line, articles as stations, hollow rings where a side stayed silent. Click an event to open its stops.

    OpenEnglish

  3. Counting Sheet

    The polling-station counting sheet on white paper with a kraft band, now without tally marks: big counted numerals per side, a hatched zero box for a blindspot, and a cross on events you've opened.

    OpenEnglish

  4. Category Standard

    The Ground News-style aggregator played straight: a top-story card with a percentage coverage bar and sources per side, a card grid with topic filters, and a blindspot rail.

    OpenEnglish

  5. Dance Score bolder

    A Labanotation-style score: one vertical centre staff, left-leaning articles as symbols to its left and right-leaning ones to its right, distance from the axis is lean, shading is certainty, a hold sign marks silence. Hover a symbol to read it.

    OpenStill

All headlines and scores are invented sample data, not real coverage.
