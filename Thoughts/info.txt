hellenicInsight (subject to change to PoliticalBro)

FEATURES
Greek news aggregator, starting with n=5 core sites.
We scrape these n sites at regular intervals, and we identify events, and we aggregate coverage of the same event from different sources. HOW
Then we feed said coverages to a judge, that classifies each one a scale from 0-1 (far-left to far-right).

The front page will display these multi-source events and clicking each event reveals a page containing the sources (having different approaches/biases) that cover it.
An objective summary of each event will be given by the app, and then the reader would choose where they would like to continue reading.
“How would you classify this article” possible option for croudsourcing and eval of our models

At the same time we also compare to our human curated samples to the oracle ones.
Assuming margin of error is small enough, we have a satisfactory way of labeling our dataset
